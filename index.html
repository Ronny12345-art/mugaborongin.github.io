👋 About Me

I am Mugabo Rongin, a cybersecurity specialist and software developer interested in cybersecurity, networking, and technology.

I am the developer of MRcutter, a Windows-based LAN network control and monitoring tool designed for educational, network administration, and authorized testing purposes.

🚦 MRcutter

MRcutter is a lightweight network management and monitoring tool for Windows. It provides features such as LAN device detection, internet access control, bandwidth control, and network traffic monitoring.

MRcutter is developed by Mugabo Rongin.
